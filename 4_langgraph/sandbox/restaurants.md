# Traditional Restaurants in Lahore, Pakistan

## 1. Cooco's Den
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Lahore Fort Food Street, Shahi Mohallah, Taxali Gate, Lahore
- **Menu:** Traditional Pakistani dishes including Chicken Tawa, Karahi, and various Tikka dishes.
- **Summary:** Cooco's Den offers authentic desi food in a picturesque setting with a rooftop view. Owned by artist Iqbal Hussain, it combines culinary delights with art, featuring a quaint atmosphere and historically rich decor. The dishes are lovingly prepared, making it a unique dining experience.

---

## 2. Muhammadi Nehari House
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Mozang, Lahore
- **Menu:** Specializes in Nihari (beef, mutton, chicken, camel) with traditional bread.
- **Summary:** A renowned destination since 1986, Muhammadi Nehari House is famed for its rich, slow-cooked Nihari, served with fresh naan. The authentic taste and tender meat make it a must-visit for lovers of desi breakfasts and traditional dishes.

---

## 3. Monal
- **Timings:** Daily 7:00 PM – 11:15 PM
- **Location:** Park and Ride Plaza, Lahore
- **Menu:** A mix of traditional and fusion dishes including Mutton Karahi, Grilled Chicken, and various buffet options.
- **Summary:** Monal offers a modern dining experience with a stunning view of the city. Known for its lavish buffets and flavorful dishes that blend traditional flavors with new culinary trends, it's perfect for both casual dining and special occasions.

---

## 4. Butt Karahi
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Bedian Road, Lahore
- **Menu:** Various Karahi dishes including Chicken and Mutton Karahi.
- **Summary:** Famous for its authentic Punjabi flavors, Butt Karahi serves up delicious Karahi-style dishes that are a staple in local cuisine. Their tender meat cooked in a rich tomato gravy is a must-try.

---

## 5. Bundu Khan
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** 106-Block-K, Gulberg 3, Lahore
- **Menu:** Traditional dishes including Karahi, Biryani, and Shahi Nihari.
- **Summary:** Bundu Khan is celebrated for its authentic Pakistani cuisine, offering a delightful range of traditional dishes. The atmosphere is inviting, and the restaurant is known for its cleanliness and hygienic standards.

---

## 6. Lal Qila
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Lahore City Center
- **Menu:** Buffet featuring Mughlai, Barbecue, and traditional Pakistani dishes.
- **Summary:** Dine in a setting reminiscent of the Mughlai era at Lal Qila, where the extensive buffet features a variety of traditional dishes, including BBQ and Mughlai specialties, providing a royal culinary experience.

---

## 7. Dera
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Lahore
- **Menu:** Varied traditional cooking styles including Saag, Chicken Biryani, and Halwa Puri.
- **Summary:** Dera Restaurant prides itself on presenting authentic Pakistani flavors, from street food to family favorites. They offer a rustic ambiance perfect for a flavorful dining experience.

---

## 8. Haveli Restaurant
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Near Badshahi Mosque, Lahore
- **Menu:** Traditional desi foods with a modern twist.
- **Summary:** Famous for its breathtaking rooftop views and the blend of hearty traditional meals, Haveli creates a unique dining atmosphere that celebrates Lahore's cultural heritage.

---

## 9. Spice Bazaar
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Lahore
- **Menu:** Traditional dishes including Murgh Musallam and Balochi Namkeen Gosht.
- **Summary:** Spice Bazaar specializes in flavor-packed traditional dishes and is well-known for its attention to quality and authentic taste, drawing inspiration from traditional kitchens.

---

## 10. Salt n Pepper
- **Timings:** Not specified
- **Location:** Fazlia Housing Society, Lahore
- **Menu:** A mix of traditional and continental dishes.
- **Summary:** Salt n Pepper offers a consistent dining experience with a reliable selection of both Pakistani and international dishes, catering to a wide range of tastes in a serene setting.
